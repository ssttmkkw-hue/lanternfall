Hold the Light

The world above has gone dark. You (Y, blue) and your bot teammate (B1, green) must keep the Hearth burning for 160 rounds while three Surges of Gloomlings come for it.

  • Mine Ember from veins (red, number = Ember left) and feed it to the Hearth (+5 fuel).
  • Your lantern drains 1 per round outside Hearth light and refills inside it.
  • Gloomlings (purple, dotted path shown) drain the Hearth. Strike them: 2 hits each.

Keys: Arrows/WASD move · E mine / deliver / pick up · F strike · Q drop · Space wait · Enter lock · Z undo · T toggle quick-lock · Shift+R restart

Rounds are simultaneous and wait for you. With quick-lock on (default), each action key locks your move immediately; turn it off to choose, undo, and lock with Enter.
